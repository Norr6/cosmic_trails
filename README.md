## Cosmic trails
Cosmic Trails is a space-themed game developed by a team of high school students. It's our first game, created during a hackathon as an opportunity to explore game development,
learn new skills, and turn our ideas into something playable.

##About the game
In Cosmic Trails, players control a spaceship, move through a space environment, and interact with bouncing asteroids.

##Development
As our first game project, we've been learning as we go, dividing tasks, solving problems, and figuring out how to bring all the different parts together.

##Our experience
This project is our first step into game development. We wanted to challenge ourselves, try something new, and see what we could create together in a hackathon.

Thanks for checking out Cosmic Trails!
